Network Access Restricted

The employee attendance kiosk is restricted to authorized on-site office networks only. Please connect to the designated Computer Center Router / Wi-Fi to clock in or manage your attendance.

Why is this required?
  • To ensure biometric and time-logging integrity, time recording is limited to facility premises.
  • Guest Wi-Fi, mobile cellular data, and remote connections are disallowed by policy.
  • If you are physically in the computer center, verify your workstation's network adapter or Wi-Fi SSID.
